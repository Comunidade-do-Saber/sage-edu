## Becoming a Maintainer

Regular, high-quality contributors who show sustained commitment to a
module may be invited to join as co-maintainers, see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md). 
Each module repository may have a designated maintainer, there is currently no
formal nomination process; interested long-term contributors should reach
out via suporte@comunidadedosaber.ao.

## Note on Project Continuity

To avoid single points of failure, the project aims to have at least two
people with administrative (owner) access to the GitHub organization and
to each module repository. This section will be updated as additional
maintainers are added.
